Book Clarity recognizes the confidential nature of the financial, business, and personal information provided by Client in connection with the Services (“Client Information”).
Book Clarity will maintain reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards designed to protect Client Information against unauthorized access, use, disclosure, alteration, or loss.

2. Remote Work and Authorized Devices
Client acknowledges that Book Clarity operates with a remote workforce and that authorized employees and contractors may access Client Information from appropriately secured, personally owned or company-approved computers and devices.
Book Clarity maintains minimum security requirements for devices used to access Client
Information, including supported operating systems, current security updates, active endpoint or malware protection, device firewalls, secure authentication, screen-lock protections, and device encryption where supported.
3. Access Controls
Book Clarity will seek to limit access to Client Information to employees, contractors, and service providers who reasonably require such access to perform the Services or support
Book Clarity's business operations.
Book Clarity employees and contractors are required to use individually assigned accounts where reasonably available and to comply with Book Clarity's applicable confidentiality, authentication, device-security, and data-handling requirements.
4. Third-Party Service Providers
Client acknowledges and authorizes Book Clarity to use reputable third-party technology and service providers as reasonably necessary to provide, administer, secure, or support the
Services.

7. Client Responsibilities
Client is responsible for maintaining reasonable security over Client's own accounts, devices, systems, employees, and credentials.
Client agrees to:
Protect passwords, authentication codes, and account credentials;
Enable multi-factor authentication where reasonably available and recommended;
Maintain accurate authorized-user information;
Promptly remove access for former employees or unauthorized users under Client's control;
Notify Book Clarity promptly of suspected unauthorized access, compromised credentials, fraud, phishing, or other security incidents that may affect Book Clarity's
Services or Client Information;

Maintain appropriate security over Client-controlled accounting, banking, payroll, tax, and financial systems.
Client will not knowingly provide Book Clarity personnel with another individual's credentials when the applicable platform permits the creation of separate authorized-user access.
8. Security Incidents
If Book Clarity becomes aware of a security incident involving Client Information under Book
Clarity's control, Book Clarity will take reasonable steps appropriate to the circumstances to investigate, contain, and remediate the incident.
Book Clarity will provide notification to Client when required by applicable law or when Book

Client agrees to reasonably cooperate with Book Clarity in responding to security incidents involving systems or accounts controlled by Client.

Client acknowledges that no computer system, cloud service, communication method, network, device, or security program can guarantee complete security or eliminate all risk of unauthorized access, cyberattack, data loss, service interruption, or other security incidents.
